## First 30-day order

1. Confirm the second member/subscriber and second director with a professional.
2. Build the minimum AI prototype and document the problem, user, dataset/license, metrics, and milestones.
3. Prepare a genuine Madhubani registered-office evidence package.
4. Review MCA incorporation inputs and current portal requirements with a CA/CS.
5. Incorporate if the professional review is satisfactory, then apply for DPIIT recognition if eligible.
6. In parallel, contact Bihar and approved incubator routes; do not call repayable support a grant.
7. Submit a small IndiaAI/C-DAC/incubator compute proposal only after recording the workload and benchmark plan.

## Source-maintenance rule

When a route changes, update its support type, stage, source URL, verification date, current-call status, and eligibility notes together. Keep amounts and deadlines out of this file unless the current official notice is archived or directly linked and the date is recorded.
